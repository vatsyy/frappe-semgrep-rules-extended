# 27: Observe the pilot on real scheduled and milestone scans

**What to build:** Exercise the admitted pilot through one real Karam milestone
and one fresh scheduled Frappe or ERPNext Compatibility Scan, then preserve,
suspend or roll back enforcement from observed evidence.

**Blocked by:** 08: Schedule weekly Compatibility Scans; 26: Integrate authorised
catalogue-based Karam gating.

**Status:** proposed — requires a real authorised Karam milestone.

- [ ] The milestone records a complete local working-tree scan and independent
      committed-tree CI scan with exact, privacy-safe provenance.
- [ ] A fresh scheduled public Compatibility Scan records findings, target
      coverage, performance and affected corpus evidence read-only.
- [ ] False positives, unresolved blockers, reviewer cost, exception expiry and
      unexplained Scan Health regressions are explicitly assessed.
- [ ] A harmful or stale blocker selects the tested last-known-good Enforcement
      Set and continues advisory until corrected and re-approved.
- [ ] Successful blockers retain immutable approval and observation history;
      monitor and audit outcomes remain non-blocking.
- [ ] Corpus Refresh records its trigger, reused and stale cases, holdout impact,
      discovery channels, next due date and any missed weekly run without rewriting.
